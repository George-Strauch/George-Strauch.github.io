Summary

I'm a software engineer at Charter Communications, where I've worked on network automation since 2022. I design and build the internal systems that automate network equipment design and the configuration that follows an install. I own their architecture across the back-end services, the APIs and the interfaces people use.

I think in architecture, APIs, data flows and integration points. My requirements come straight from the field technicians and network engineers who use what I build.

Before Charter I studied applied mathematics and computer science at Texas State University. My undergraduate research there in machine learning and computer vision led to a paper published through IEEE.

Approach

A large share of my code now starts as a draft from a language model. I use LLMs and coding agents to write and refactor code, find my way around unfamiliar codebases, research problems and write tests.

They're fast, and they can be wrong in ways that look right. So more of my job is now deciding what to build and giving the model the context and constraints it needs. Then I read what comes back carefully and own the result.
